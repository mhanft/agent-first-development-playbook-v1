Format: index first, entries below. Same shape: lesson → context → what to do differently.

## Entries

### 1. A brand name is locked only when domain, social handles, and trademark are all verified
A name is a *candidate* until domain (authoritative WHOIS), social handles (the platforms you'll use), and trademark (the relevant registries, in your class) are all confirmed available. Soft signals like a Google search are not proof.

### 2. Enable cloud billing/cost export on Day 1
Cloud providers don't backfill billing exports — data accrues only from enablement forward. Enable it at project bootstrap or permanently lose early cost history.

### 3. Track wait-time separately from work-time
Banking, government registration, and payment-processor approval block launch but consume no building time. Track calendar-bottlenecked items separately from engineering hours so estimates stay honest — work time ≠ wait time.

### 4. Payment processors distrust new domains
Processors silently drop verification emails to domains under ~90 days old. Sign up with an established email first, then add the brand email as secondary.

### 5. Customer-facing copy stays silent on how fulfillment works
Whether work is done by a human or an agent is not the customer's concern — they care about the result. Keep operational mechanics out of customer-facing copy.

### 6. Quality over speed — no artificial launch deadline
Shipping a half-built customer journey to hit a date creates worse outcomes than shipping later and whole. Sequence work so the pipeline can deliver before the front door opens.
